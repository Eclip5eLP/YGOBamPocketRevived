# Series 3 card proposal (2026-11-01)

Goal: Series 3 about the size of Series 2 (567 cards: 416 monsters, 151 spells). Theme: the Synchro (5D's) and Xyz
(ZEXAL) era, since Series 3 brings Synchro and Xyz summons. Source: the YGOPRODeck download (14,653 cards); counts
leave out cards already in the game (by name) and card kinds the game can't use (Link, Pendulum, Skill, Token).

How the game's card kinds map: the game has monsters and spells only. Tuner, Synchro and Xyz work as built
(notes/synchro_xyz.md); Fusion monsters use the original fusion rules; Traps would become spells (continuous spells
cover most trap-like effects). Effects are re-made from the game's six effect pieces; card art comes from the
download.

**How to choose:** pick archetypes (and later standalone cards) until about 570 cards. An archetype doesn't have to be
taken whole; "Cards" is everything available. Suggested core (my pick, about 330 cards): Junk/Synchron/Stardust, Red
Dragon Archfiend, Black Rose, Blackwing, Ice Barrier, X-Saber, Utopia/Numbers, Gagaga & friends, Heroic,
Photon/Galaxy, Inzektor, Madolche, Gem-Knight. The remaining ~240 from other archetypes plus standalone Synchro/Xyz/
Tuners and generic monsters/spells.

Headliners = the strongest Synchro/Xyz/Fusion (by ATK) in the group; some groups include later prints of the same
theme (e.g. Stardust Sifr Divine Dragon), which can be left out.
