## Yugioh BAM Pocket Revived v1.1.0

The big update: live duels against your friends, tournaments, the lost booster packs and cards, and the whole
game in the app. Install the APK below on a 64-bit Android phone. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your
progress. Version 1.0.0 still plays offline but needs this update to play online.

### New
- **Live duels**: challenge an online friend to a real-time duel from the friends menu or their profile.
  Challenges pop up wherever you are in the game.
- **Tournaments**: live duels move you up and down the ranks to Elite; the Elite leaderboard shows the top 100.
- **Works out of the box**: the original game content ships inside the app. A new install needs no download and
  plays offline straight away; only newer content downloads.
- **Booster packs re-added**: *Wings of Destiny*, *Blazing Wind* and *Immemorial Cataclysm*, the three series 2
  packs the original game never sold.
- **About 280 cards re-added**, which the game showed as "Coming soon": in those packs, in the card shop as you
  clear story chapters, or as Arena and tournament rewards.
- **The God cards**: Obelisk the Tormentor at tournament rank 10, The Winged Dragon of Ra at rank 5.
- **Weekly Arena rewards** for the top 25 players.
- **Collection**: every card in the game, the ones you own first, and each card's popup says where to get it.
- **Profiles**: use any card you own as your picture, change your name and password, see other players'
  profiles and decks, and copy a deck you like.
- **New friends menu**: search for players, a list of all players, friend requests with a "new" mark on the main
  menu, remove friends, and a green dot on friends who are online.
- **Delete decks** from the deck chooser.
- **Faster and lighter**: the game starts about 15 seconds faster, uses far less network traffic, and keeps
  your settings across updates.

### Fixes
- The map no longer jumps back after you drag it.
- The shop no longer shows the server popup every time.
- A new booster pack's first fusion card now shows the pack animation, and the fusion tutorial can be finished.

### Notes
- The APK is larger (about 120 MB) because the game content is now inside it.
- Weekly team rewards and Elite end-of-tournament rewards are not handed out yet.

Full setup and self-hosting guide in the [README](https://github.com/Eclip5eLP/YGOBamPocketRevived#readme).
